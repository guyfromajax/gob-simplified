Breaks

Breaks boost every drill and reduce fatigue into the next game. Too many strain chemistry and weaken Fight and Discipline. Strong-chemistry teams absorb more downtime.

Scrimmages

Scrimmages sharpen execution and multiply the installs you run. They tend to lift chemistry. An intense scrimmage can boil over, and over-scrimmaging risks fatigue.
