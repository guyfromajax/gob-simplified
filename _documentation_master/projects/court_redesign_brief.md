Update the design of the 120 non-confernce 1 teams' court images to add variability, elegance and authenticity to the images.

##Inspiraiton
Use the 8 conference 1 teams as inspiration for what is possible. Bentley-Truman, Morristown, Ocean City, Xavien, Little York, South Lancaster, Lancaster, and Four Corners

They have a different texture to the hardwood (I'd like to vary this a bit), some have team logo marks on the court either at center court or on deep wing locations inr inside the 3 pt arc, some have team name along the baseline, the have refletions of lights overhead (I'd like to very this a bit).

Overhead-light reflections should inherit the slightly imperfect, human placement seen on the Conference 1 courts. When reflections are used, introduce only very subtle variation in spacing, vertical alignment, dimensions, intensity, and softness; never make the row mechanically exact or exaggerate the irregularity enough to look accidental.

## Approved rollout decisions

- Abilene is included in the 120 non-Conference-1 court redesigns.
- Full logo/mascot wordmarks must not be rotated into the baseline border bands.
- Baseline branding is tabled for the current rollout and should not be added to production courts. The earlier 10% maximum and Bentley-Truman direction remain a possible future option if the typography treatment is revisited.
- A team logo normally appears only once on a court. The approved non-center 2-logo and 4-logo options explicitly override this rule and repeat the logo in the selected symmetric locations.
- Other decorative locations may remain unbranded instead of repeating the logo.
- A team wordmark may appear only at center court. It may use either the school/team name or the mascot name.
- A center-court wordmark may be used with no team logo anywhere else on the court.
- A center-court wordmark may also be paired with one non-repeated team logo elsewhere on the court.
- IDA is the single approved exception to the logo uniqueness rule and retains its five shield placements as an intentional organic outlier.
- Twenty new courts may use one wordmark in a single deep-wing position, in addition to Ocean City. These are narrow exceptions to the center-only wordmark rule. Randomly vary these between school/team-name and mascot-name wordmarks, and independently vary the placement among the upper-left, lower-left, upper-right, and lower-right deep-wing positions.
- "Deep wing" means the open midcourt channel outside a three-point arc and between that arc and the center-court line, using Ocean City's lower-right school-name placement as the reference. It does not mean the corner/short-wing area between an arc and a baseline.
- Roll the center-court, non-center treatment, and hardwood design independently and randomly. Repeated valid combinations are allowed; apply compatibility rules after rolling.
- All listed percentages are per-team random-selection weights, not portfolio quotas or required final totals. Do not rebalance later courts to force the completed set to match the percentages exactly; natural variation is expected (for example, parquet does not need to finish at exactly 5%).
