

**What Are Practice Squads?**
- After week #1 training camp each season, you'll need to assign 3 of your players to your practice squad.
- This will bring your active roster down from 15 to 12.

**Practice Squad Rules**
- Once a player is assigned to your practice squad, he will be unavailable for the rest of the season, but he will be available to your team for the next season.

**Practice Squad Games & Season**
- Practice Squad players and Recruits will compete in a Practice Squad Season.
- Practice Squad teams are built by Region. All recruits in each region and teams' practice squad players will be put into a pool and allocated to one of six teams.
- Each team plays two games against each team at the same level from the other regions (14 regular season games total).
- There will be a seeded 8-team tournament at the end of the season.
- The All-Americans champion will play the All-Stars champion to crown the ultimate Practice Squad Champion.
- You can see Practice Squad game results, box scores, team standings, and player season stats via links in the News section of your Command Center.

**Practice Squad Teams Talent Tiers**
1. All-Americans (top 12 players in the region)
2. All-Stars (the next 12)
3. Varsity (the next 12)
4. JV (the next 12)
5. Squad (the next 12)
6. Scrubs (all remaining players, some may not get any playing time)

**Who Should You Assign To The Practice Squad?**
- Your least talented players, or if you have a younger player who you think won't get much playing time on your varsity team and you want to see him get a lot of playing time against similar competition.
- Players will develop attributes while on the practice squad team. They will get more playing time, so that will increase their development relative to being on varsity if they're not set to get a lot of playing time on varsity. But it will be against lesser talent, so playing time at this level is less developmental than playing time on the varsity team.
