

**Tradeoffs**
- Benefits
    - You'll generate more turnovers and offensive fouls.
    - Your opponent will fatigue faster.
    - Opportunity to generate uncontested or lightly contested Fast Break shots off steals.
    - Opportunity for momentum-building outcomes (steals, open fast break baskets).

- Drawbacks
    - You'll commit more defensive fouls.
    - Your players will fatigue significantly faster (defense fatigues more than offense).
    - You run the risk of giving up broken-trap fast breaks to your opponent.
    - Sub-par player abilities can be a significant headwind -- it's hard to hide weak defenders in a trap or press defense.

**Tips & Hints**
- Build your team's Press/Trap attribute if you plan to run these defenses a lot. It makes a difference.
- Conversely, if you expect a lot of presses and traps from your opponents, build your Press/Trap Offense team ability.
- The area inside the 3-point line is the offense's safe area. It's where they're looking to get the ball in order to push a Fast Break or set up the Half-Court Offense.
- Defenses look to force the offense into trappable situations -- most notably the areas above or below the 3-point line and either near the half-court line or baseline.
- The offense's main goal will be to get the ball inside the 3-point area. Their second goal will be to get to the middle of the court, as that will enable multiple opportunities to then get inside the 3-point area and break the trap.
- Your goal with presses and traps is to force the issue. There is no sense in running these defenses if you want to play it safe, as the risk/reward with this is significantly dialed up compared to standard Half-Court Offense situations.
- A safe setting for these on the gameplan screen is 1 -- it will allow you to mix in some presses and traps without overly fatiguing your team. But if you have a team built to press and trap, then obviously, lean into it.
- Use the Playcall Center -- you can override your presets with the Press/Trap setter in the Playcall Center. This is ideal if you have a player getting tired or getting into foul trouble and want to turn presses and traps completely off. Or if you're sensing weakness in your opponent to handle these -- dial them up!!

**HCT Play Details**
- Standard Trap
    - Three backcourt defenders look to force the offense into trappable situations.
    - Two backcourt players toggle across the area to protect the rim and prevent passes into the safe zone.
    - Relies on a strong central backcourt defender (usually your PG, but you will be able to customize this in the near future).
    - Also relies on two intelligent frontcourt defenders (usually your PF & C, but you will be able to customize these in the near future) to minimize the number of wide-open offensive looks at the basket.
- Straight Pressure
    - This is the one HCT defense where very few trap attempts occur. This is more focused on intense man-to-man pressure starting at the half-court line by the three backcourt defenders.
    - The two frontcourt defenders still execute safety zone defense in the area inside the 3-point line.
    - Ideal if you feel you have a simple man-on-man advantage over your opponent in the backcourt and you don't want to take unnecessary risks.
- Standard Diamond
    - The riskiest of the three HCT defenses. It has the highest likelihood of extreme outcomes favoring both the offense and the defense.
    - Relies on a fast (read: high AG) frontcourt defender (usually your SG, but you will be able to customize this in the near future) to cover the immediate area near the basket from top to bottom.
    - Puts your slowest defender (usually C, but you will be able to customize this in the near future) right at the top of the key area to defend pass attempts to the middle. This is your best chance to mask a slow defender in an HCT defense, but he has to be smart or he'll be a liability here reading passes and cuts to the middle.
    - Your other three defenders will need strong outside defense and agility in order to cover the area they need to cover and execute traps.
