Advanced Topic

PRESS & TRAP DEFENSE

Presses and traps are your high-risk, high-reward defenses. Run well, they create turnovers and wear your opponent down — but the risk/reward is dialed way up, so they reward coaches who commit rather than play it safe.

Tradeoffs
Benefits
  • You'll generate more turnovers and offensive fouls.
  • Your opponent will fatigue faster.
  • Opportunity to generate uncontested or lightly contested fast break shots off steals.
  • Opportunity for momentum-building outcomes (steals, open fast break baskets).
Drawbacks
  • You'll commit more defensive fouls.
  • Your players will fatigue significantly faster (defense fatigues more than offense).
  • You run the risk of giving up broken-trap fast breaks to your opponent.
  • Sub-par player abilities can be a significant headwind — it's hard to hide weak defenders in a trap or press defense.
Tips & Hints
  • Build your team's Press/Trap attribute if you plan to run these defenses a lot. It makes a difference.
  • Conversely, if you expect a lot of presses and traps from your opponents, build your Press/Trap Offense team ability.
  • The area inside the 3-point line is the offense's safe area. It's where they're looking to get the ball in order to push a fast break or set up the Half-Court Offense.
  • Defenses look to force the offense into trappable situations — most notably the areas above or below the 3-point line and either near the half-court line or baseline.
  • The offense's main goal is to get the ball inside the 3-point area. Their second goal is to get to the middle of the court, which enables multiple opportunities to then get inside the 3-point area and break the trap.
  • Your goal with presses and traps is to force the issue. There's no sense in running these defenses if you want to play it safe — the risk/reward is significantly dialed up compared to standard Half-Court Offense situations.
  • A safe setting for these on the gameplan screen is 1 — it lets you mix in some presses and traps without overly fatiguing your team. But if you have a team built to press and trap, then obviously, lean into it.
  • Disciplined teams execute presses and traps more effectively.
  • Teams with Fight break through presses and traps more effectively.

Half-Court Trap Play Details

Each HCT defense assigns your five defenders differently. Pick based on your personnel and how much risk you're willing to take on.

ST
Standard Trap
Balanced
  • Three backcourt defenders look to force the offense into trappable situations.
  • Two backcourt players toggle across the area to protect the rim and prevent passes into the safe zone.
  • Relies on a strong central backcourt defender (usually your PG, but you'll be able to customize this in the near future).
  • Also relies on two intelligent frontcourt defenders (usually your PF & C, customizable in the near future) to minimize wide-open offensive looks at the basket.
SP
Straight Pressure
Lowest Risk
  • The one HCT defense where very few trap attempts occur. It's focused on intense man-to-man pressure starting at the half-court line by the three backcourt defenders.
  • The two frontcourt defenders still execute safety zone defense in the area inside the 3-point line.
  • Ideal if you feel you have a simple man-on-man advantage over your opponent in the backcourt and you don't want to take unnecessary risks.
SD
Standard Diamond
Highest Risk
  • The riskiest of the three HCT defenses. It has the highest likelihood of extreme outcomes favoring both the offense and the defense.
  • Relies on a fast (read: high AG) frontcourt defender (usually your SG, customizable in the near future) to cover the immediate area near the basket from top to bottom.
  • Puts your slowest defender (usually C) right at the top of the key area to defend pass attempts to the middle. This is your best chance to mask a slow defender in an HCT defense — but he has to be smart, or he'll be a liability reading passes and cuts to the middle.
  • Your other three defenders will need strong outside defense and agility to cover their area and execute traps.
