

**Context**
Leading up to each game, the user will see a Scouting Report tab in the Command Center with details of their next opponent.

**Scouting Report Tab**
- Opponent Name, Record, National Rank, & link to opponent's team page.
- Projected Starting Five
- Team Measures -- all eleven core Team Attributes including a spider chart
- Play Usage from last game

**Opponent's Team Page**
- Shows player attributes and player stats




**Tips & Hints**
- Study the Scouting Report tab in the Command Center and opponent team page before running training each week, so you have context on how you want to manage training.
- Look for tendencies from your opponent. Do they run a particular play more than others? Do they run Motion or Set Plays more often on offense? Do they run Zone or Man more often on defense. Do they like to Fast Break, Press or Trap more often than normal? Who is their top scorer or scorers? 
- Look for potential mismatches in your opponent's projected starting 5 and players who get a lot of playing time off the bench. Do you have a potential advantage down low with inside plays? Or maybe from a depth standpoint that would allow you to push the pace a bit more with Fast Breaks? Or do they lack the athletes to defend an attack-heavy offensive scheme with lots of drives to the basket?
