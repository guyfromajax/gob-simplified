Generated banner — three directions

Card format, 400 × 141, the §2 convention. Each direction is shown at 1× and 2×, across three name lengths so the type behaviour is visible rather than assumed. Swap palettes to check the treatments hold on colours that aren't flattering.

Palette

A — Chevron

§6.2 as written. Angled split from primary to a darkened primary, secondary as a chevron edge running bottom-left to top-right. Ghost initials bleed off the left and bottom at ~12% opacity. Wordmark centred, mascot beneath in wide small caps. The accent bar follows the diagonal instead of sitting flat.

B — Band

The wordmark sits on a solid horizontal band that interrupts the diagonal, so the name reads at any size and against any palette. Ghost initials are larger still and bleed off the right. Riskier at 1× — the band eats vertical space — but the most legible of the three on a busy screen.

C — Blade

A single hard diagonal, no chevron. Ghost initials sit large and low-contrast behind the wordmark rather than beside it, so the name is always the subject. The accent is a thin blade tracking the split. Closest to the general fallback's logic — scale and crop carrying the composition, not detail.

All three are parametric — colours, school name, mascot and initials are inputs, so any of them renders for all 128 programs plus a custom one. Type auto-fits: the wordmark shrinks to fit its box rather than overflowing, which is what makes South Lancaster and IDA sit in the same frame. Output here is SVG for review; the shipped generator draws to canvas per §6.1.
