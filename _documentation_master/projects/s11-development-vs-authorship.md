## Resolution

Development no longer undoes authorship. Coaching owns shape; identity is current shape (floors, not a mean pull); `OFFSEASON_ATTRACTOR_ALPHA` is retired (level-only offseason). Grow level, coach shape.

## What §11 originally asked

Whether Team Builder authorship survives development. If the sim pulls authored attributes toward a target the user didn't choose, the roster they built decays — and TB becomes cosmetic after year one.

## What the first measurement found (pre-framework)

Control **0.147** · realistic **0.150** · extreme **0.147** retained profile-deviation at graduation.

Authorship was not being uniquely erased. **Individuality was** — and the same α-blend hit all 128 programs every offseason. That reframed the question as reshape vs grow (league-wide), not a TB-only fix.
