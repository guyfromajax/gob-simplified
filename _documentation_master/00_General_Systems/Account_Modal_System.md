        -Bottom Row: "Headshot"
     -Point 3: 
        -Top Row: "Jersey #"
        -Bottom Row: "Position"
     -Point 4: 
        -Top Row: "Position"
        -Bottom Row: "Jersey #"
    -Sprite Dispay
        -Shows image, positiion (player's highest RT position), and/or jersey # of the user team's top rated player, based on the toggle setting that the toggle is on.
