# SEO Keyword Research — Geeked Out Basketball

Quick research based on what ranks and what people search for in your space. **You don’t have exact search volume here** — for that, use [Google Keyword Planner](https://ads.google.com/home/tools/keyword-planner/) (free with a Google Ads account) or a free tier of [Ubersuggest](https://neilpatel.com/ubersuggest/) and plug in the phrases below.

---

## What’s out there (who ranks)

| Phrase / theme | Who shows up | Notes |
|----------------|--------------|--------|
| **basketball simulation game** / **basketball sim** | Basketball GM, Zen GM, WhatIfSports, Basketball Sim (mobile), browser-based lists | Strong “management sim” intent; mostly NBA/GM, not high school. |
| **basketball management game** | Basketball GM, Grizzled GM, Zen GM | Same as above — GM/roster focus. |
| **basketball coach game** / **basketball coach simulation** | Be The Coach (college), DriveTheLane (college), bball-coach.com, Basketball Coach | Smaller niche; “coach” + “sim” = strategy/play-calling, not arcade. |
| **high school basketball game** / **high school basketball simulation** | Often NBA 2K used for high school sims, or generic “basketball games”; few dedicated high school sims | Your angle (high school program builder) is under-served; less volume, less competition. |

So: **“basketball simulation game”** and **“basketball sim”** have clear demand and established competitors (Basketball GM, etc.). **“Basketball coach game”** has fewer dedicated results. **“High school basketball simulation”** / **“high school basketball coach game”** are more niche — good for differentiation once you have a bit of authority.

---

## Recommended focus (homepage)

- **Primary (1):** **basketball simulation game**  
  - Use in: meta title, one main heading or subhead, first ~100 words.
- **Secondary (2):** **basketball coach game**, **basketball sim**  
  - Use naturally in body copy and maybe one more heading; don’t stuff.
- **Supporting (use when it fits):**  
  - **high school basketball simulation** / **high school basketball game** (differentiator).  
  - **strategy basketball game**, **basketball management game** (supporting only).

Keep the homepage focused on the primary + secondaries. Use “high school” and “coach” in copy so you’re relevant for those searches without trying to rank the homepage for 10 phrases.

## How to get more volume data (optional)

1. **Google Keyword Planner** (free): Add a Google Ads account, go to Tools → Keyword Planner. Enter:
   - basketball simulation game  
   - basketball sim  
   - basketball coach game  
   - high school basketball simulation  
   - high school basketball coach game  
   You’ll see estimated monthly searches and competition (for ads; still useful for SEO).
2. **Google.com manual check:** Search each phrase and look at:
   - **“People also ask”** and **“Related searches”** at the bottom — add those to your list if they fit.
3. **Ubersuggest (free tier):** Enter your domain and the phrases above to see volume estimates and “keyword ideas.”

Use volume to confirm which of the secondaries gets more traffic (e.g. “basketball sim” vs “basketball coach game”) and to spot any other low-competition phrases worth a dedicated page later.
