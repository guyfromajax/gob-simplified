## 1. The squad mark

- **What it is:** a square with the squad's Region letter, and the tier name in small capitals under it.
- **Small sizes (your call today):** below 40px the mark is the letter alone. At 18px (beside a name in a list) no tier name is readable, and the squad's full name is always next to it. The tier name shows on the team page banner (72px).
- **Tier names found in the data:** All-Americans, All-Stars, Varsity, JV, Squad, Scrubs (`BackEnd/practice_squad/constants.py`, and confirmed on the real save). The mark reads the tier from the squad's own name ("Region A All-Americans"), so it shows whatever the data calls it.
- **Does the longest fit?** "All-Americans" did not fit the 72px banner mark in the body face, so the tier line uses the condensed display face (the one the headings use). All six names fit, at 9px; tested.
- **One component, tokens only:** `practiceSquadMarkHtml` in `common.js`, styled by `.gob-squad-mark` in `gob-tables.css`. Colours: `--surface-2` fill, `--line-strong` edge, `--text-100` letter, `--text-60` tier. No new colours.

## 2. The sweep

**The cause.** A practice squad is not a program and has no art. Every logo lookup goes by team name through `getTeamAssetPath` (`common.js`); an unknown name fell through to the generic set, `/images/teams/general/…`, and the generic square is the same knight as Bentley-Truman's.

**The fix, at the source.** `getTeamAssetPath` now recognises a squad's name or id first: it returns the generated letter mark for a logo, and an empty image for any banner or background. `GOBTables.markHtml` (the shared mark every list uses) draws the squad component. So no surface, present or future, can fall back to a program's art for a squad.

## 3. The roster split

**Where Starters come from.** The route (`GET /franchise/practice-squad/team`) builds the Projected Starting Five with the shared selector (`projected_starting_five_from_payload`, the same one autoset uses) and the team page marks those five as Starters.

**Why only 2 came through.** The route handed the selector each player with his **season** stats in a flat `stats` field. The selector reads a flat `stats` as **this game's** line, so season fouls were read as fouls in a game in progress: five or more in a season meant "fouled out", and the selector seats only as many positions as it has eligible players. A squad with three players under five season fouls got three Starters; as the season goes on the count falls (you saw two). It also picked the wrong five even when five were left, since anyone with fouls was passed over.

Evidence from the real save (week 3, two squad games played): Region A All-Stars had season fouls of 2, 4, 4, 5, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 8, 8, 9: exactly three under five, and exactly three Starters. Two of the 40 squads were short.

**Fix.** The route selects the five on the players without their season line (a five at tip has no fouls). The season line is still sent and shown. No change to the selector, the sim, or any other caller (the varsity callers nest the season under `stats.season`, so they never had this).

**Result on the real save:** all 40 squads: 12 players, 5 Starters in PG, SG, SF, PF, C order, 7 Bench. Each Starter's POS column shows the position he starts at.

**Fewer than 12, or no five?**

| Case | How often (real save) | What shows |
|---|---|---|
| 12 players, five seated | 40 of 48 squads | 5 Starters, 7 Bench |
| Scrubs (tier 6), empty roster | 8 of 48 (every region's Scrubs had no weekly roster stored yet) | "0 players", an empty roster. Scrubs are not on the Practice Squads table, so there is no link to these pages. |
| Fewer than five players | None found | The selector seats as many slots as there are players, from PG down; the rest of the page is unchanged. No new rule invented. |

## 4. submit-cuts flake

The page plays a checkbox's sound through a dynamic import, so it lands a moment after the click; the test cleared its sound list too soon and counted the late sound against the next step. The test now waits for the sounds already on their way (4 after the four ticks, 1 after the untick, 3 in the desktop test) before clearing. Test only; no product change. Before: 4 failures in 40 runs. After: 60 of 60.
