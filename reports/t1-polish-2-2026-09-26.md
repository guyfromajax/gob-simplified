## Logo root cause

The broken icons on the offline Team Stats shot were missing files, not a bad path and not a remote URL.

`getTeamAssetPath(name, 'logo_square')` asks for `/images/teams/<slug>/<slug>_logo_square.png`. Those nine teams (Lawrence, Lexington, Quigley Catholic, Melbourne Americas, Empire City, Kenton, GP Prep School, Syracuse, Houston Jesuit) are in `CORE_TEAM_ASSET_SLUGS`, so the client does not fall through to `general_logo_square.png`. The loopback serves that path from the static tree: the request is a 307 to `/static/images/teams/<slug>/<slug>_logo_square.png`, and that file 404s.

129 team folders sit under `FrontEnd/static/images/teams/`. 77 of them have no `*_logo_square.png`. 73 of those 77 have a `logo_primary.png` (and usually a wordmark). Four have no logo file at all: Cupertino, Decatur DEI, Empire City, HA Rushmore. Lancaster and `general` do have `logo_square.png`, which is why some rows looked fine.

That is not a one-line desktop path fix. Pointing every known slug at `general` would hide the logos that do exist, and `logo_primary.png` is a different asset (Empire City has neither). Generating the 77 squares is packaging work. `desktop/README.md` now has a packaging checklist item for it. The tables ship a monogram until those files exist.

## Monogram

Standings and Team Stats already shared `GOBTables.teamLink` and `GOBTables.nextCell`. That helper now draws the mark:

- No logo URL renders a monogram immediately. No `<img>`.
- An image that errors is replaced by the same monogram. The broken `<img>` does not stay in the DOM.
- The letter is the first character of the team name. The background is the team's `primary_color` (hex only; anything else uses `#27408E`). The chip is 18×18, the same box as the logo.

Team Stats did not send `primary_color`. The existing teams lookup now includes it. Standings already had it, including for the NEXT opponent when that opponent is in the loaded list.

Rankings rows, Leaders rows, and Office standings / next game do not render a team image. On the offline save they had no broken icon: Rankings 0 images, Leaders team cells are abbreviations (portraits still load), Office standings and next game are text. National standings had 154 monograms and 0 broken images. Team Stats had 77 monograms (one per team missing `logo_square`) and 0 broken images. Conference standings in Lancaster's conference had logos and no monograms. Region had 2 monograms.
