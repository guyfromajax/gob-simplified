# T3 player and team detail — 2026-09-27

Player and team detail are drill-ins inside the command center. A list pushes the view. Back returns to that list with its scroll. The page formats the payload. It does not derive a rate, rank, place, streak, or attribute bucket.

The page `team_id` stays the user's team. The team being read is `view_team_id`. Putting the viewed team in `team_id` would make Office treat that team as the user.

## Pager keys

Documented in UX_System.md §14.

- `gob-view-roster-order` — JSON array of player ids. `pager=roster`.
- `gob-view-leaders-order` — same shape. The full list writes the visible rows. A board card writes that card. `pager=leaders`.
- `gob-view-standings-order` — `{ ids, label }`. `label` is the conference card, for example `A2`. `pager=standings`.
- No `pager` param means no pager.
- Prev is disabled on the first row and Next on the last. Paging replaces the history entry.

The rail highlights `origin` (`team`, `league`, `office`, `prep`). A deep link with no origin uses Team when the player or team is the user's, and League otherwise. These views are not sub-tabs.

## Frame fields the routes do not send

Left off the page, not computed:

- Hometown, starter/bench role on the player hero, a game log, and prior seasons. The stats table is the current season line and the career line.
- Tip time, a clock time, and a neutral site. Next game is `at York, Week 25` from `next_game.site`, `opponent_name`, and `week`.
- PTS and REB columns on the frame's compact roster. The team page reuses the roster attribute grid. `GET /roster` does not send per-game rates.
- A "Trains ST" line beyond `development.emphasises`. Standard has an empty list, so that line is omitted.
- Scout them, unless this team's `next_game.opponent_id` is the user's team and `coaches-tab` is on the page. The link pushes the scouting report.
