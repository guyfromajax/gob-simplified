## Game Plan

The range inputs are five-stop `.gt` tracks. The ids are unchanged (`slider-offense` and the rest), the values are still 0–4, and the POST `/api/gameplan` body is unchanged. Each track is `role="slider"` with `aria-valuemin`, `aria-valuemax`, `aria-valuenow`, and `aria-valuetext` (the stop's label, or "between X and Y" for stops 1 and 3). Arrow keys step, Home and End jump, and a click or drag on the track moves the knob. Stops 1 and 3 are unlabeled. The tick words are the existing three spans.

Effect lines live in `EFFECT_LINES` in `game-plan.js`:

- offense: Motion freedom vs designed set plays.
- inside: Paint touches. Your highest-percentage shots.
- attack: Drives and cuts. Draws the most fouls.
- outside: Perimeter shots. Lowest %, most impact.
- tempo: Push for early shots or work for a good one.
- alterations: Follow the script or read and react.
- defense: Man wins matchups. Zone saves legs and fouls.
- aggression: More steals, more fatigue and fouls.
- hc_trap: More turnovers. Faster fatigue, more fouls.
- fc_press: Bigger disruption than a trap, bigger cost.
- fast_breaks: How hard you push in transition.
- rebounding: Second chances vs getting back on D.

The shot-diet nest keeps the existing tip. The offense-not-all-Never modal is unchanged. Save Game Plan is the orange button in the tab-row tools. The new CSS is the block in `game-plan.css` that starts at the "Prep v2 game plan" comment. It adds no tokens. A search of that block finds no hex and no `rgb()`. Save uses `var(--orange)`.
