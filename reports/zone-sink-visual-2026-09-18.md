# Zone sink — candidate weight sweep (Stage A)

Where the five defenders stand **when their own zone is empty** — that is rung (e), and rung (e) only.
Rungs 0/a/c/d position a defender against a real offensive player and are untouched; a defender whose
zone contains someone never reaches this code. So read every panel as "if nobody were in his area".

`CURRENT` is today's behaviour (`_find_closest_spot_in_zone_to_point`: snap to whichever LISTED SPOT is
nearest the ball). The other four are `GOB_ZONE_SINK` candidate presets.

Grid: x 62-92 left to right at 2 units per character, y 4-46 bottom to top at 2 units per row
('upper' spots are the top of the panel), offense attacking the rim on the right (`R`).
`o` = ball. Defenders `1`=PG `2`=SG `3`=SF `4`=PF `5`=C, drawn OVER the ball marker.
**Two defenders in the same cell overwrite each other — read the coordinate table under each panel row,
which is exact.** Stacking is itself a finding and is called out where it happens.

Nothing is landed: `GOB_ZONE_SINK` is default OFF and flag-off is byte-identical to the reference.
Read this with `reports/zone-sink-2026-09-18.md`.
