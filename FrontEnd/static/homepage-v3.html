Frequently Asked Questions

What is Geeked Out Basketball?

Geeked Out Basketball is a basketball simulation game where you build and coach an elite high school program from the ground up. It's a strategy-focused basketball sim — you develop players, set game plans, build playbooks, and make decisions during live gameplay.

Is this a basketball sim or an arcade game?

It's a basketball sim, not an arcade game. You don't control players in real time — you coach. Set your system, manage your roster, call plays, make halftime adjustments, and answer to the press after every game.

What makes this basketball simulation different?

Most basketball games are either arcade action or basic GM sims. Geeked Out Basketball sits in a different category entirely — deep tactical coaching, real player development, a full 26-week season, and a post-game press conference system that makes every decision feel meaningful.

Is Geeked Out Basketball free to play?

The alpha is free to play in your browser — no download, no install. Sign up and you're coaching.

Who is this basketball simulation game for?

Coaches and sim fans who want depth and strategy. If you've ever wanted a basketball game that respects your basketball IQ, this is it.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Geeked Out Basketball is entirely browser-based. Desktop recommended for the best experience.
