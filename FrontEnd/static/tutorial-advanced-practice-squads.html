PRACTICE SQUADS

Each season you'll trim your roster down to a 12-man varsity squad — and the three players you set aside don't just disappear. They play a full season of their own, develop their attributes, and come back to you next year.

  • After Week #1 training camp each season, you'll need to assign 3 of your players to your practice squad.
Practice Squad Rules
  • Once a player is assigned to your practice squad, he'll be unavailable for the rest of the season — but he'll be available to your team again the next season.
Practice Squad Games & Season
  • Practice Squad players and Recruits compete in a Practice Squad Season.
  • Practice Squad teams are built by Region. All recruits in each region and teams' practice squad players are put into a pool and allocated to one of six teams.
  • Each team plays two games against each team at the same level from the other regions — 14 regular season games total.
  • There's a seeded 8-team tournament at the end of the season.
  • The All-Americans champion plays the All-Stars champion to crown the ultimate Practice Squad Champion.
  • You can see Practice Squad game results, box scores, team standings, and player season stats via links in the News section of your Command Center.

Who Should You Assign To The Practice Squad?
  • Your least talented players — or a younger player who won't get much playing time on your varsity team and who you'd like to see get a lot of run against similar competition.
  • Players develop attributes while on the practice squad. They get more playing time, so that increases their development relative to riding the varsity bench. But it's against lesser talent — so playing time at this level is less developmental than playing time on the varsity team.
The Takeaway

Use the practice squad as a development tool, not just a cut list. A promising young player buried on your varsity bench will often grow faster getting real minutes against practice-squad competition.
