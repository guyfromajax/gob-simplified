FAQ

Questions?

Everything you need to know about Geeked-Out Basketball before you get on the court.

What is Geeked Out Basketball? +

Geeked Out Basketball is a basketball simulation game where you build and coach an elite high school program from the ground up. It's strategy-focused — you develop players, set game plans, build playbooks, and make decisions during live gameplay.

Sim or arcade game? +

It's a basketball sim, not an arcade game. You don't control players in real time — you coach. Set your system, manage your roster, call plays, make halftime adjustments, and answer to the press after every game.

What makes this different? +

Most basketball games are either arcade action or basic GM sims. GOB sits in a different category — deep tactical coaching, real player development, a full 26-week season, and a post-game press conference system that makes every decision feel meaningful.

Is it free to play? +

The alpha is free to play in your browser — no download, no install. Sign up and you're coaching.

Who is this for? +

Coaches and sim fans who want depth and strategy. If you've ever wanted a basketball game that respects your basketball IQ, this is it.

Do I need to download anything? +

No. Geeked Out Basketball is entirely browser-based. Desktop recommended for the best experience.
