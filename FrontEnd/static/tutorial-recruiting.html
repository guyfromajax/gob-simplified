EVERY RECRUIT HAS LEANS

Before anything else, you need to understand leans. The whole recruiting system runs on them. Every recruit has a lean list: the schools he's leaning toward, ranked by preference. He can have up to three leans. You'll find every recruit's leans under Coaching Resources → Recruits.

Each step up the list roughly doubles your odds of signing that recruit.

Nothing is locked in until Recruiting Day. Leans shift all season — a #1 slot in November is momentum, not a guarantee.

Loyal lean

When your program holds the #1 lean slot, a recruit is genuinely favoring you. Protect it with wins and strong visits — but know the list can still move.

Locked in

A lean is interest, not a contract. Recruits only commit when you run Recruiting on Week 35 — that's when points and promises turn into signatures.

THE RECRUITING CALENDAR

Recruiting breathes with the season — long passive stretches, a focused invite window, another wait through the tournament, then the payoff.

Weeks 1–19

Passive Phase

Passive
  • Wins and quality losses can add your program to a recruit's lean list.
  • Performance shifts who recruits are watching — no invites yet.
  • Build relationships quietly before the window opens.
Weeks 20–26

Invite Season

Active
  • Save recruiting orders before each training week.
  • One official visit per team per week — make each one count.
  • A strong visit can move you up a recruit's lean list.
Weeks 27–34

Tournament

Passive
  • Postseason play — recruiting waits for the national tourney to finish.
  • Wins and losses still shift leans in the background.
  • Hold your closing pitch for Recruiting Day.
Week 35

Recruiting Day

The Payoff
  • Assign recruiting points and playing-time promises.
  • Run Recruiting to sign your class.
  • This is when commitments become real.
Strategy

RECRUITING TIPS

Win in Your Region

Home-region recruits lean toward local programs — stack wins when it matters most.

Use Your Visits

Weeks 20–26 are your only official visit window — prioritize recruits already leaning your way.

Read RT & Archetype

RT tells you talent today; archetype tells you fit — don't chase stars who don't match your system.

Get on the Lean List

Everything else gets easier once a recruit is leaning your way — that's the job all season long.
