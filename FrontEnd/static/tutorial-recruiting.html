RECRUITING

How you build your program — from the first lean of the season to the madhouse of Recruiting Day.

Start Here

EVERY RECRUIT HAS LEANS

Before anything else, understand leans — the whole system runs on them. Every recruit has a lean list: the schools he's leaning toward, ranked by preference. He can have up to three leans. You'll find every recruit's leans under Coaching Resources → Recruits.

Each step up the list roughly doubles your odds. Being a recruit's top choice matters enormously — but a lower spot, or no spot, can still be overcome.

Nothing is locked in until Recruiting Day. Recruits show and change leans all season. But Recruiting Day is a madhouse and anything can happen.

Loyal lean

Leaning toward a school before a single game is played — a legacy, or he grew up following that program. Hard to flip.

Locked in

Holds a single lean all season long. You can still win him over with enough effort, but treat him as a tough target.

Reading a Recruit

TALENT, AT A GLANCE

A recruit's rating tells you how much raw talent you're looking at — but it's only the starting point.

Limited Potential0-29
Role Player Potential30s
Starter Potential40s
Elite Potential50+

A freshman's rating before training camp is roughly half of his finished potential. A 45 could develop into a ~90-overall senior — or stay a 45. Some recruits explode; some never climb. Recruiting is a gamble.

The Season

THE RECRUITING CALENDAR

Recruiting breathes — two passive stretches where you win games and let leans come to you, and two active windows where you take action.

WKS 1–19 · Passive Phase
WKS 20–26 · Invite Season
WKS 27–34 · Tournament
Passive — you win games, leans come to you Active — you take action Recruiting Day — the payoff
Weeks 1–19

Passive Phase

Passive
  • Recruits declare leans on their own, driven by how teams perform.
  • Beat higher-ranked opponents to earn declarations; bad losses work against you.
  • Your Recruits tab tracks everyone who has you on their list.
Weeks 20–26

Invite Season

Active
  • Invite one recruit per week — 7 total. Stack-rank a list of up to 20.
  • A visit likely earns a lean; winning the game he attends boosts it a lot.
  • Mix in some safe targets when you send invites each week — a wasted week with no visit is the worst outcome.
Weeks 27–34

Tournament

Passive
  • No active recruiting, but leans still shift.
  • Tournament games carry far more weight than early-season ones.
  • Advance deep and momentum compounds; get eliminated and you risk getting passed.
Week 35

Recruiting Day

The Payoff
  • Build a 20-recruit list (prefilled with your leans); every name on your list gets a call on Recruiting Day, and is therefore in play to choose your team.
  • Spend 20 bonus points however you like — go all-in on one player, spread thin, or focus on a few key targets.
  • Playing-time promises are powerful but binding: if you promise, you have to play him or your program will suffer.
Strategy

RECRUITING TIPS

Recruit In-Region

Players near home are likelier to accept invites and sign — they want to stay close. Focus your energy there.

Mine the Mid-Tier

Find lower-rated players who fit your system and show them love — fill your roster with real talent, not walk-ons.

Winning Is Everything

Wins build the aura — and your prestige — that make recruits want to play for you and accept your invites.

Get on the Lean List

It's the single best predictor of landing a recruit. Everything else on this page is built to get you there.
