PLAYBOOKS

Your playbook sets the mix of presets the sim will choose for every possession — and the plays you can call in real time to override those presets, via the Playcall Center.

Orientation

Playbook Editor

Where you build and edit everything. Reach it from the Edit Playbooks button on the Command Center.

Command Center Tab

The Playbooks tab shows your current setup at a glance — read-only, no editing.

During a Live Game

Pull up your playbook as a read-only reference any time from the Lineup screen.

Playbook Components

Usage Percentages

Your automatic mix

The presets the sim uses to call plays each possession — whether you're playing live or simming. Each play type totals 100% on its own:

Motion (offense)100%
Set Plays (offense)100%
Man (defense)100%
Zone (defense)100%
Fast Breaks (holistic mix)100%

The balance between Motion and Set Plays — and between Man and Zone — is set separately in your Game Plan. Think of these presets as your team's default tendencies: what they'll naturally run when you're not calling something yourself.

Playcall Center

Your real-time control

Holds the plays you can call on demand during a game to override your presets — up to 8 offensive plays and 8 defensive plays. You can set these plays in your Playbook Editor.

Offense · up to 8
1 Base Post Play (C)
2 Pick & Roll - Iso (SG)
3 Double Screen Three (SG)

One useful quirk: a play can sit at 0% usage and still live in your Playcall Center — perfect for a situational play you never want called automatically but want on hand.

Playcall Center Override Behavior
One turn
Offensive playcalls

Call a play, it runs that possession, then you're back to your usage mix.

Persists
Defense, Press/Trap, Tempo & Aggression

These hold until you change them — or until a timeout, quarter break, or player foul-out resets things.

Managing Your Team's Command of the Playbook

Command operates on a 0–100 scale. There are benefits to pushing this score above 100, but at a diminishing scale.

Training raises it

The more your team practices a play in Training Installs & Scrimmages, the higher its Command climbs.

Calling it lowers it

The more you run a play in live games, the more its Command drops — you're putting it on tape for opponents to scout.

That's the tension to manage: leaning on your best plays makes them more readable over time, while practice keeps them sharp.
