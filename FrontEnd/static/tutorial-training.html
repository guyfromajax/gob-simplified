Scrimmages

Scrimmages sharpen execution and reinforce system cohesion — they have a multiplying effect on the Installs you put into place during Team Drills. They also tend to improve team chemistry, but that is not always guaranteed as sometimes your players can come to blows during a particularly intense scrimmage. Over-scrimmaging also runs the risk of your players starting the next game a bit fatigued.

Coaching Style

Your coaching focus determines what you emphasize across drills, scrimmages, post-practice wrap-ups, and pre-game locker room talks — shaping motivation, tone, and where your team grows most. For maximum impact, align it with the drills you run that week.

Leaning into one primary category builds a clear identity. Pairing it with a strong secondary focus compounds gains as the season progresses — shaping not just development, but who your team becomes.
