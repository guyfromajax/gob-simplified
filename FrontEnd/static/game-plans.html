GAME PLANS

The dials you set before every game — how your team attacks, defends, and controls tempo.

Offense

Offense
100% Motion 50/50 100% Set Plays

Determines your mix of motion offense vs structured set plays. Leaning toward motion increases read-and-react freedom. Leaning toward sets emphasizes designed execution and called actions.

Inside
Never Normal Most

Controls how frequently you prioritize inside scoring. This influences both the number of Inside Set Plays you call and how aggressively your team looks to establish paint touches within motion offense. All things being equal, inside shots tend to be your most high-percentage shots.

Attack
Never Normal Most

Controls how often your team looks to generate attack shots — drives, cuts, and downhill pressure. A higher setting increases Attack Set Plays and pushes motion offense toward rim pressure. Attack shots tend to draw the most defensive fouls, and are best executed by your most athletic players.

Outside
Never Normal Most

Controls how frequently your team prioritizes perimeter scoring. This affects both Outside Set Plays and your shot selection tendencies within motion offense. Outside shots are your lowest percentage shots, but they tend to have the most impact on the game.

Fast Breaks
100% Half-Court Sets 50/50 100% Fast Breaks

Determines how aggressively your team pushes in transition. Higher settings increase fast break attempts. Lower settings favor entering structured half-court offense.

Defense

5
Defense
100% Man 50/50 100% Zone

Determines your mix of man-to-man vs zone defense. Adjusting this shifts how your team protects the rim, contests perimeter shots, and handles defensive rotations.

Man defense generates more individual matchups and high-impact plays. It's ideal if you have disciplined defenders who can win their assignments and recover quickly.

Zone defense offers structural advantages. Players fatigue less, commit fewer fouls, and receive more consistent help support. However, zones are more vulnerable to perimeter shooting and can concede offensive rebounds due to the lack of fixed box-out assignments. Zone schemes are also executed more effectively by high-IQ defenders who anticipate and rotate instinctively.

Aggression
Passive Normal Aggressive

Controls how aggressively your team attempts to create disruption. Higher aggression increases steal attempts and playmaking risks across the floor. It also increases fatigue rate and foul likelihood.

Half-Court Trap
Never Normal Most

Determines how frequently your team executes half-court traps. Trapping increases turnover potential and can disrupt offensive chemistry, but defenders fatigue faster and are more prone to fouls.

Full-Court Press
Never Normal Most

Determines how frequently your team applies full-court pressure. Presses amplify turnover potential and disruption more than half-court traps, but also carry greater fatigue cost and foul risk.

Rebounding
100% Crash the Boards 50/50 100% Get Back on D

Determines how aggressively your team attacks the offensive glass. Higher settings send more players to crash for second-chance opportunities. Lower settings prioritize defensive balance and transition coverage.
