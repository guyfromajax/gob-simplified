Frequently Asked Questions

What is Geeked-Out Basketball?

Geeked-Out Basketball is a strategy-first basketball sim game built for coaches, not button mashers. It's a true basketball simulation where you manage rotations, build game plans, recruit & develop players, and navigate full seasons from preseason training through the postseason. Every possession is driven by logic, tendencies, and coaching decisions — not arcade mechanics.

How realistic is this basketball simulation?

This is a deep, system-driven basketball simulation designed to mirror real coaching strategy. Player attributes, energy, chemistry, matchups, and game plans all impact outcomes. If you think in terms of defensive schemes, shot selection, and development arcs, this basketball sim rewards that mindset.

How is Geeked-Out Basketball different from NBA 2K?

NBA 2K is an action game with simulation elements. Geeked-Out Basketball is a pure basketball sim. You don't control players directly — you control decisions. It's about coaching philosophy, roster construction, in-game adjustments, and long-term program building. If you care more about strategy than stick skills, this is your game.

What game modes are available?

Geeked-Out Basketball includes multiple modes, including Scrimmage, Tournament Mode, and our crown jewel Franchise mode. You can build a roster under constraints, manage training, set tactical emphasis, and battle through a full competitive season in this basketball simulation game.

Can I customize coaching strategy and player development?

Yes. This basketball sim game is built around strategic control. You can adjust offensive and defensive tendencies, assign training focus areas, manage energy, and shape player growth over time. Your coaching ability directly impacts how your team performs.

Do I need to know basketball to play?

You don't need to know plays by name, but you'll get more out of it if you think like a coach. The game rewards basketball IQ — understanding matchups, managing player minutes, adjusting at halftime. The deeper your knowledge, the deeper the game gets.

Is Geeked-Out Basketball a basketball management game?

It's more than a management game — it's a coaching simulator. While you manage roster decisions and development, the core of the experience is strategic basketball logic. If you're looking for a realistic basketball management sim that prioritizes on-court decision-making, this is it.

Is Geeked-Out Basketball multiplayer?

The current alpha focuses on delivering a polished single-player basketball simulation experience. Competitive and expanded multiplayer features are part of the long-term roadmap as the game continues to evolve.
